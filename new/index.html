Questions

What is Keep 30?

A 30-day challenge inside FireKeep. Every day you keep six small rules: your workout on plan days, protein, water, steps, sleep and one proof photo.

Is it like 75 Hard?

It's built on the same idea, daily rules and a streak, but made for real life: rest days count, short days count, and missing one doesn't wipe your progress.

Is FireKeep free?

Yes. FireKeep is free on iPhone and Android.

Do I need equipment?

No. Start with bodyweight and a chair. Add a band or use a gym when you're ready.

I'm over 50 / a beginner. Is it for me?

Yes. You pick your level and your days, and every move is shown clearly. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.

FireKeep is a fitness app and does not give medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.
