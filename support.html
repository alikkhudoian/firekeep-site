FireKeep Support

Need help? Email hello@firekeep.app and we will reply within 2 working days.

Where is my data?

On your phone only. FireKeep has no account in this version. Deleting the app deletes your data. If you back up your iPhone (iCloud or a computer), the backup can include FireKeep’s data. That backup is yours; we never see it.

How do I change my plan?

Open Train, tap your plan, and change your days, place or equipment.

Is FireKeep medical advice?

No. FireKeep is a fitness app. Check with your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition plan.
